    How a car
    moves through.

    From the first message to handing back the keys. The order is the same whether it's a front-end PPF or a full colour change.

    01

    Message us

    WhatsApp or call with the car, what you want done, and a couple of photos. We'll tell you if it's a fit and roughly what it costs.

    02

    Inspection

    Bring the car to Strømsveien. We check the paint under light, point out chips and swirls, and confirm the price and dates.

    03

    Deep clean

    Foam wash, iron and tar removal, clay. Film and coatings only stick properly to paint that is completely bare.

    04

    Prep & correct

    Polishing where the paint needs it. For wraps, badges, handles and trim come off so the film can tuck behind edges.

    05

    Install

    Film is laid, stretched and squeegeed panel by panel, or the coating goes on section by section under the lights.

    06

    Cure & check

    Edges are post-heated, coatings cure, and the whole car gets checked in full light before anything goes back together.

    07

    Handover

    We walk you round the car and explain how to wash it and how long to wait before the first wash.

    Before you book.

    PPF or ceramic coating — which do I need?

    They do different jobs. PPF is a physical film that stops stone chips and scratches. Ceramic is a hard, glossy layer that makes the car easier to clean and keeps the shine, but it won't stop a chip. A lot of owners do PPF on the front and ceramic over the whole car.

    Will a wrap damage my paint?

    On factory paint in good condition, film comes off cleanly when you want it gone. Resprayed or damaged paint can lift during removal, which is why we check the paint at the inspection before quoting.

    How long will my car be with you?

    It depends on the car and the job. A polish can be a day, a full colour change or full-body PPF takes longer. We'll give you dates when we confirm the booking. add typical times

    Can I wrap a leased car?

    Usually yes, because the wrap protects the paint and can be removed at the end of the lease. Check your lease agreement first, some ask you to tell them.

    How do I wash it afterwards?

    Hand wash with a pH-neutral shampoo and skip automatic washes with brushes. Wait a few days before the first wash so film edges and coatings can settle. We'll give you the exact aftercare at handover.

    Do you work on electric cars?

    Yes, a lot of what you'll see on our Instagram is electric. Tell us the model when you message and we'll take it from there.

    Is there a warranty?

    Warranty depends on the film or coating used. Ask us when you get your quote and we'll tell you exactly what's covered. to confirm
