Color Paint Protection Film

What is
Color PPF?

A protective film with pigment or a colour effect, fitted to the car’s painted surfaces.

A new colour — and physical protection

Unlike clear PPF, which keeps the car’s original colour, Color PPF gives the car a new look. It combines a colour change with physical protection, made to lower the risk of stone chips, light surface scratches and wear from normal use.

Color PPF is also called PPF colour film, coloured paint protection film or colour change with PPF.

Benefits of Color PPF

  • Changes the car’s colour without a permanent respray
  • Helps protect the original paint from stone chips
  • Lowers the risk of light scratches and daily wear
  • Available in many colours and finishes
  • Can be self-healing, depending on the film
  • Can be removed professionally when you want a new look
  • Styling and paint protection in one

Color PPF does not give complete protection against every kind of mechanical damage. Deep scratches, hard impacts and damage through the film can still happen.

Why Color PPF in Norway?

Norwegian roads are hard on paint — stone chips, gravel, sand, road salt and dirt all year round. Color PPF gives the car a personal look and lays a protective layer over the original paint, which makes it a strong option for owners who want both styling and protection.

  • New cars that should get a personal look
  • EVs like Tesla, BMW and Polestar
  • Sports cars and premium cars
  • Owners who want another colour without a respray

Color PPF or
a vinyl wrap?

Both change the car’s look, but they have different properties.

Color PPF — styling and paint protection

Color PPF is made to combine a colour change with physical protection of the paint. It is usually thicker than traditional vinyl and can resist stone chips and some kinds of mechanical wear better. Many Color PPF films also have a self-healing top layer.

Which should you choose?

Choose Color PPF if you want a colour change and paint protection together. Choose a vinyl wrap if you mainly want to change the look and want the widest range of designs. We offer both and help you pick the right film for the car.

Durability and care

Keep the colour
looking new.

Lifetime depends on film type, quality, use, care and the conditions the car lives in.

Up to 10-year manufacturer warranty

We offer selected PPF films with up to a 10-year manufacturer warranty, depending on the film series and the manufacturer’s terms.

The warranty only covers the conditions set by the manufacturer and does not automatically cover stone chips, accidents or wrong care.

Caring for Color PPF

  • Wait at least 48 hours after fitting before the first wash
  • Use car care products suitable for PPF
  • Avoid harsh chemicals and abrasive cleaners
  • Don’t aim a pressure washer straight at the edges from close range
  • Remove bird droppings and insects as soon as you can
  • Follow the advice for your film type

Matte and satin films can need different products than gloss films.

Questions about
Color PPF.

Ask us directly
Does Color PPF protect against stone chips?

Yes. Color PPF is made to protect the paint physically and lowers the risk of stone chip damage. How much depends on the film and the size of the impact.

What is the difference between Color PPF and normal film?

Color PPF combines a colour change with paint protection; traditional vinyl is mainly for styling and design. PPF is usually thicker and better at resisting stone chips.

Can Color PPF be removed?

Yes, normally professionally from original paint in good condition. On repainted or damaged surfaces the risk of paint damage is higher.

Is Color PPF better than a vinyl wrap?

It depends on what you want. Color PPF is a good choice if you want protection and a new look together; a vinyl wrap can be the better option if you mainly want a colour change.

Can I wash a car with Color PPF?

Yes, with suitable products and methods. Wait at least 48 hours after fitting and follow the care advice for your film.

Can I get matte, satin or metallic Color PPF?

Yes — gloss, matte, satin and metallic, among others. Availability varies between manufacturers and film series.

How long does Color PPF take to fit?

A full wrap normally takes 3–4 days, depending on the car, the film and the scope.

Can ceramic coating go on Color PPF?

Yes, if the coating suits that film type — it makes cleaning easier. On matte and satin films we use products that don’t change the finish.

From NOK 45,000 for a sedan, from 53,000 for an SUV and from 58,000 for a van. These are starting prices — the price goes up with the size of the car, the film and the work involved. Send make, model, year and the colour you want for a quote.
