Vinyl or PET?

Ask us directly
What is the difference between vinyl and PET film?

Vinyl gives great freedom in colours and design. PET film can give an extra smooth, mirror-like surface with impressive colour depth, depending on the film type.

Which films do you wrap with?

We wrap with vinyl and PET film, including 3M Wrap Film Series 2080 and Avery Dennison Supreme Wrapping Film. You can try the colours on a car in the 3D studio above. Can’t find the colour you want? We can order it for you.

How much does a wrap cost?

Car wrapping starts from NOK 27,000 for a sedan and NOK 33,000 for an SUV. These are starting prices — the price goes up with the size and shape of the car and how advanced the job is. Chrome delete, stripes, fleet graphics and vans are priced after we've seen the car. Send us the car and what you want on WhatsApp.

Are the colours on screen exact?

They're a guide. Screens show colour differently, and metallic, pearl and flip films change with the light. We show you the real film sample in the studio before we start.

Can you put our company logo on the car?

Yes. We've done vans and trucks like the DHL service-partner van and Akershus Logistikk's trucks. Send us your logo and the vehicle.
