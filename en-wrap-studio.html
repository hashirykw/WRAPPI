    About the
    studio.

    Are these the real film colours?

    Yes — every colour carries the manufacturer's own name and code from the 3M 2080 and Avery Dennison Supreme Wrapping Film ranges. Screens show colour differently, so we show you the real sample in the studio before we start.

    My car isn't in the studio — does that matter?

    No. The cars show how a colour or design looks on a body shape close to yours. Tell us your exact model when you send the build.

    Does my uploaded logo get sent anywhere?

    No. It stays in your browser. Send it to us on WhatsApp when you want a quote.

    How much does a wrap cost?

    Wraps, stripes and fleet graphics are priced after we've seen the car. PPF, Color PPF and polishing have fixed price-list figures — see the price list.
