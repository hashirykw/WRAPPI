Where are you?

Strømsveien 324, 1081 Oslo, at Furuset by IKEA Furuset. Look for the Wrappi sign on the black building.

How do I get a price?

Send us the car model and what you want done on WhatsApp, or use the price builder on the price page and we write the message for you.

Yes. Most of the cars on our price list are electric, from the ID.3 and IONIQ 5 to the Model Y and ID. Buzz.
