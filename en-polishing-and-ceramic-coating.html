Car polishing

Give the car
its gloss back.

Has the paint lost its gloss? Seen wash marks, small scratches or a dull, tired surface? We improve the paint, reduce visible scratches and bring back a deeper, clearer gloss.

What is car polishing?

We use polishing machines, the right compounds and pads to improve the surface of the paint. Controlled machine polishing can reduce or remove many surface scratches, wash marks, oxidation and other minor paint defects.

The result can be a smoother surface, better light reflection and a more intense gloss. Polishing is also an important part of the prep before ceramic coating.

What can polishing help with?

  • Wash marks and light surface scratches
  • Swirls — circular wash marks
  • Dull, lifeless paint
  • Light oxidation
  • Holograms from earlier polishing
  • Minor surface defects
  • Low gloss and uneven reflection

How much can be corrected depends on how deep the scratches are, the condition of the paint and how much can be polished safely. Deep scratches through the clear coat normally can’t be removed by polishing.

Ceramic coating · Koch Chemie

Ceramic
coating.

A car that is easier to keep clean, has a great gloss and is better protected against daily wear. You get a Koch Chemie certificate with checks at 12, 24 and 36 months.

What is ceramic coating?

Ceramic coating — also called ceramic paint protection or ceramic sealant — is a liquid treatment applied to the paint that cures into a thin, hard-wearing protective layer.

Many ceramic coatings are based on silicon compounds, often called SiO₂ technology. The treatment makes the paint hydrophobic, so water beads and runs off — and dirt, road dust and other contaminants come off more easily in a normal wash. Compared with traditional wax it usually lasts longer and resists chemicals better.

Why ceramic coating?

  • Easier washing — more water-repellent, less dirt that sticks
  • Protection against road salt and chemicals — an extra layer against salt, traffic film and tar
  • Deeper gloss — a clearer shine that brings out the original colour
  • Long-lasting protection — normally lasts longer than traditional wax
  • Less waxing — less time on maintenance through the year

Regular washing is still needed, especially in winter. The coating doesn’t make the car self-cleaning, but it makes dirt stick less and washing easier.

Especially useful in a Norwegian winter

Rain, snow, road salt, sand and traffic film make a car dirty fast. Ceramic coating is a good option for owners in Oslo, Alnabru, Lørenskog, Lillestrøm and Romerike who want easier care and a tougher surface.

On a new car — and on a used one

Even a brand-new car can have small wash marks, transport contamination or other unevenness. We always check the paint and clean and prep it first, with a light polish if needed. On a used car with wash marks or reduced gloss, polishing may be needed for the result you want.

Ceramic coating does not remove existing scratches — any paint correction should be done before the coating.

Durability and care

How long
does it last?

Durability varies between products — some are made for shorter protection, others can last several years with the right care.

What affects how long it lasts

  • How often the car is driven
  • Whether it is parked outside or in a garage
  • Exposure to road salt, sunlight and pollution
  • How often and how the car is washed
  • Which car care products are used

Ceramic coating or wax?

Both give gloss and water repellence. Ceramic coating normally lasts longer, sheds water well and makes care easier; wax gives temporary protection and a nice gloss, but has to be applied more often.

Does it stop scratches and stone chips?

Ceramic coating doesn’t give mechanical protection. With the right washing technique it can help reduce wash marks, but it doesn’t stop stone chips, deep scratches or hard impacts.

Caring for a coated car

  • Regular, gentle washing
  • Products made for ceramic coatings
  • Remove bird droppings and insects quickly
  • No abrasive cleaners
  • Clean microfibre cloths and gentle wash kit
  • Respect the curing time before the first wash

Ask us directly
What is the difference between 1-step and 2-step polishing?

1-step combines light correction and gloss improvement in one step. 2-step has a correction stage and a finishing stage for a more thorough result.

Does polishing remove every scratch?

No. It can reduce or remove many surface scratches, but deep scratches through the clear coat normally can’t be polished out.

Can a brand-new car be polished?

Yes. Even new cars can have wash marks or small defects. We check the paint before we recommend polishing.

How often should a car be polished?

There’s no fixed interval — it depends on the paint, how the car is washed and how much wear it gets. Correcting too often should be avoided.

How long does a polish last?

Polishing improves the surface, but new wash marks can appear with use. The right wash technique and paint protection help keep the result.

Is ceramic coating worth the money?

For many owners, yes — it makes the car easier to look after and helps keep a nice paint surface. The value depends on the car’s condition, use and the protection you want.

Does the car have to be polished before ceramic coating?

Not always. If the paint has scratches, wash marks or reduced gloss we may recommend polishing first. The paint always has to be cleaned and prepped properly.

Does ceramic coating make the car scratch-proof?

No. It doesn’t make the car scratch-proof and doesn’t protect against stone chips or hard mechanical damage.

When can I wash the car after ceramic coating?

Yes, once the coating has had its curing time. The exact wait depends on the product.

Can ceramic coating be removed?

Yes, but a cured coating can need machine polishing or other professional treatment to remove it completely.
